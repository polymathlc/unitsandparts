How do I spot a Simultaneous Concept question?

You are given the values of 2 sets of things from which you can form 2 equations. Once you have them, your job is to remove or replace one unknown so only one is left to solve. There are two main strategies:

🟡 Elimination

Match the coefficients of one variable in both equations (using ×, ÷, or simplification), then subtract one equation from the other to make that variable disappear.

🔵 Substitution

Build an exchange rate between the two unknowns and use it to swap one unknown for the other. Solve in terms of just one variable.
